See what a report contains

Three of the figures a typical biomarker report includes, drawn here from illustrative data. Your report carries the statistics behind each one and the code that made it.

Does the marker predict survival?

What it shows
Overall survival for patients with high and low expression, with censored patients marked and the hazard ratio from a Cox model.
In your report
Univariate and multivariate Cox tables adjusted for stage and age, cut-off method, and numbers at risk.

Can it tell tumour from normal?

What it shows
How well a single marker and a combined panel separate the two groups, summarised as the area under the curve.
In your report
AUC with 95% confidence intervals, sensitivity and specificity at the chosen cut-off, and validation in an independent cohort.

Did expression change in your samples?

What it shows
Log2 fold change for each target after normalisation, with error bars and significance from the appropriate test.
In your report
Reference gene stability, sample quality flags, the full ΔCt table and the statistical test used for each target.

Free tools for researchers

Open-source apps for analysis problems we see again and again. Free to use, validated against independent calculations, with the code on GitHub.

miRNA qPCR Analyzer

For QuantStudio and Bio-Rad CFX exports

Upload your instrument export as it is. The app checks sample quality before it calculates anything, then gives fold changes, statistics and a publication-ready figure.

  • Hemolysis check for serum and plasma (miR-23a / miR-451a)
  • Spike-in recovery to catch failed extractions
  • Reference gene, spike-in or global mean normalisation
  • 44 automated tests against independent calculations
Open the qPCR app View the code

KAP Survey Analyzer

For knowledge, attitude and practice studies

Upload a raw questionnaire export from CSV or Excel. The app scores each domain, runs the statistics journals expect and writes a Word report with three-line tables.

  • Answer-key scoring, Likert mapping and Bloom's cut-offs
  • KR-20 and Cronbach's α reliability with confidence intervals
  • Chi-square, group comparisons and logistic regression with adjusted odds ratios
  • Data processed in memory and never saved
