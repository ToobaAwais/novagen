    Does the marker predict survival?

    What it shows
    Overall survival for patients with high and low expression, with censored patients marked and the hazard ratio from a Cox model.
    In your report
    Univariate and multivariate Cox tables adjusted for stage and age, cut-off method, and numbers at risk.

    Can it tell tumour from normal?

    What it shows
    How well a single marker and a combined panel separate the two groups, summarised as the area under the curve.
    In your report
    AUC with 95% confidence intervals, sensitivity and specificity at the chosen cut-off, and validation in an independent cohort.

    Did expression change in your samples?

    What it shows
    Log2 fold change for each target after normalisation, with error bars and significance from the appropriate test.
    In your report
    Reference gene stability, sample quality flags, the full ΔCt table and the statistical test used for each target.

    Recent work

    Two examples of the projects we deliver. Client details are kept confidential.

    Liquid biopsy, diagnostics company

    Tissue-of-origin signals in urine miRNA

    The question
    Can a tissue-specific miRNA score detect pancreatic signals in urine, and how much of the signal comes from the lab kit rather than the biology?
    What we did
    Ran a tissue-scoring pipeline on a 417-sample urine cohort, developed an approach to separate kit and library-prep effects from true biology, and compared paired urine and plasma samples across tissues and tumour-microenvironment cell types.
    Outcome
    Delivered over three fixed-price milestones, each reviewed and approved by the client's scientific team.
    • Urine and plasma miRNA
    • Batch effects
    • Tissue atlases
    • Python
    Cancer biomarkers, published study

    miRNA biomarkers for liver cancer progression and survival

    The question
    Which tissue and circulating miRNAs track progression and survival in hepatocellular carcinoma?
    What we did
    Analysed TCGA-LIHC tissue data alongside independent GEO cohorts, testing differential expression, stage associations and survival for each candidate.
    Outcome
    Published in the peer-reviewed journal Non-coding RNA Research in 2026.

    Questions clients ask

    If your question isn't here, send it with the form below.

    How is the price set?

    Each project has a fixed price based on the agreed scope. You approve the analysis plan and the price in writing before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

    How is my data kept confidential?

    We work with anonymised data only, never with patient identifiers. A confidentiality agreement can be signed before you share anything.

    What if our data cannot leave the hospital?

    We can develop and test the analysis on a sample or a simulated dataset, then give you documented code to run on your own system.

    What file formats do you accept?

    FASTQ, count matrices, 10x Genomics outputs, instrument exports, Excel, CSV and SPSS files. If yours isn't listed, ask.

    How long does a project take?

    It depends on the data and the scope. The timeline is part of the analysis plan you approve, and you're told straight away if a data problem affects it.

    Will I be able to reproduce the results?

    Yes. Every project includes the documented code or notebook and a technical summary of each step and parameter, so you or a reviewer can rerun everything.
