Clinical research statistics

Statistical support across a clinical study, from sample size and design to the analysis, meta-analysis and the re-analyses reviewers ask for.

Who it's for

  • Hospitals and research centres without an in-house statistician
  • Residents and faculty with clinical or survey data to analyse
  • Teams answering statistical reviewer comments on a submitted manuscript

What you receive

Study design and sample size

You receive: Power and sample size calculations, a statistical analysis plan for your protocol or ethics application, and an independent review of an existing design.

  • Power analysis
  • Analysis plans
  • Protocol review
  • Randomisation

Clinical statistics

You receive: Descriptive tables, the right test for each question, regression and survival models, and journal-format tables with a full record of every test.

  • Regression
  • Survival analysis
  • Mixed models
  • Propensity scores

Meta-analysis

You receive: Pooled effect sizes, heterogeneity and subgroup analysis, sensitivity and publication-bias checks, with forest and funnel plots ready for your PRISMA-style report.

  • Random effects
  • I² heterogeneity
  • Egger's test
  • Forest plots
  • Diagnostic accuracy

Reviewer response support

You receive: The re-analyses reviewers ask for, corrected tables and figures, and a technical log of each statistical change, so your team can write the response letter.

  • Re-analysis
  • Sensitivity analysis
  • Revised figures
  • Methods check

Data we accept

Anonymised data in Excel, CSV or SPSS, plus your protocol or research questions. For meta-analysis, your extraction sheet. We work with anonymised data only, and a confidentiality agreement can be signed before you share anything.

How a project runs

  • You share your study and the data you have.
  • You receive an analysis plan with the scope, timeline and a fixed price, agreed in writing before any work starts.
  • Quality control comes first, and any data problems are reported as they are found.
  • You receive figures, tables, documented code and a technical summary of every step, organised so you can rerun everything.

Analysis, figures and tables only; manuscript writing is not included.

Questions

Can you calculate sample size before the study starts?
Yes. You receive the calculation, the assumptions behind it and the analysis plan wording for your protocol or ethics application.
What if our data cannot leave the hospital?
We can develop and test the analysis on a sample or a simulated dataset, then give you documented code to run on your own system.
Do you write the manuscript?
No. We provide the analysis, tables, figures and a technical record of every test. Your team writes the paper.
